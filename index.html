Animal Information

Discover a few amazing animals and learn quick facts about them.

Lion

Habitat: Grasslands and savannas

Diet: Carnivore

Lions are powerful big cats known for living in groups called prides.

Elephant

Habitat: Forests and grasslands

Diet: Herbivore

Elephants are the largest land animals and are famous for their memory and long trunks.

Dolphin

Habitat: Oceans and coastal waters

Diet: Carnivore

Dolphins are intelligent marine mammals that communicate using clicks and whistles.
